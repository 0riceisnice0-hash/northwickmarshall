## Generated office concept

`assets/office-concept.jpg` was generated using the built-in imagegen tool on 8 November 2026 and converted from the retained PNG to a web-sized JPEG. The public image has no caption; this document records its provenance.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: consulting website office concept image, wide landscape 3:2. Create an elegant realistic British boutique business consultancy meeting room with warm oak table, navy upholstered chairs, glass partitions, cream stone, daylight, a few green plants. Empty room, no people. In the background a dark navy feature wall with brushed brass company name reading exactly "Northwick Marshall", small widely spaced word "CONSULTING" below, and above it a tasteful geometric emblem of three interlocking outline diamond shapes, centre gold, outer two brass. Architectural editorial photography, tactile natural materials, restrained premium styling, credible small professional office rather than huge corporate headquarters. Perspective view from the table towards the wall, brand sign clearly legible yet naturally part of the room. No watermarks. This is a proposed office visual for a design prototype.

## Brand and typography

The SVG mark was redrawn against Ben's original screenshot on 10 November 2026. Its side diamonds are separated, its crossing lines have the reference's interwoven gaps, and its stroke weight is increased. `mark.svg` is the inverse version for navy surfaces; `favicon.svg` uses navy outlines for a light surface.

DM Sans is loaded from Google Fonts under its open font licence. The wordmark uses Georgia.
